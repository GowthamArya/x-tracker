Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

x-tracker helps you organize personal transactions, accounts, trips, and shared expenses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, Google account identifier, and profile picture as permitted by Google. We also collect the x-tracker accounts, transactions, categories, and shared-expense information that you create.

Gmail access

If you choose to connect Gmail, x-tracker requests read-only access to identify transaction and payment emails. We use that access only to find relevant messages, extract transaction details such as merchant, amount, date, and debit or credit type, and show those details to you for review or entry into x-tracker.

x-tracker does not send, delete, modify, or compose Gmail messages. We do not use Gmail content for advertising, sell it, or use it for unrelated purposes. You can disconnect Gmail at any time. After disconnection, x-tracker will stop accessing your mailbox; you may also request deletion of stored Gmail-related data.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure x-tracker, synchronize connected services at your request, maintain your transactions and shared accounts, respond to support requests, and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

Your information is shared only with service providers needed to operate x-tracker, when you intentionally share an account or trip with other users, when required by law, or to protect the security and rights of x-tracker and its users.

Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. OAuth credentials and tokens are intended to be protected on the server and are not exposed to other x-tracker users. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

We retain information while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can request deletion of your x-tracker account and associated personal data. Disconnecting Gmail removes the connection from future use; imported transaction records may remain until you delete them or request account deletion.

Your choices

You may review or update information in x-tracker, disconnect Gmail, and request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information. You can also remove x-tracker’s access from your Google Account security settings.

Children’s privacy

x-tracker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as x-tracker changes. The updated version will be posted on this page. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the x-tracker team through the support contact listed in the application.
